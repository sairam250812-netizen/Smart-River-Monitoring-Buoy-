About RiverGuard AI

Turning river cleanup into a smart, science-based solution.

RiverGuard AI is a floating smart monitoring and collection system designed to address the growing problem of floating waste in rivers and waterways. The project combines water-quality sensing, computer vision, AI detection, GPS tracking, and a net-based collection mechanism into one intelligent platform.

River pollution is a serious environmental challenge affecting ecosystems, drinking water, aquatic biodiversity, and public health. Plastic bottles, wrappers, and other floating waste often accumulate in rivers and create long-term damage to the environment. Traditional cleaning methods are often reactive, limited, and not continuous.

Why this matters

  • Floating waste threatens fish, birds, and other aquatic life.
  • Polluted rivers worsen water quality and biodiversity loss.
  • Manual cleaning is slow, inefficient, and difficult to scale.
  • Smart monitoring helps identify pollution sources and hotspots.

How It Works

From river patrol to waste collection.

01

Buoy movement

The floating buoy moves across the water to monitor selected river areas.

02

Sensor monitoring

Sensors continuously measure water quality and environmental conditions.

03

Camera capture

A visual camera scans the river surface for floating waste objects.

04

AI detection

AI identifies waste using image-based analysis and pattern recognition.

05

Collection mechanism

Suitable waste is lifted using a net-based system for collection.

06

GPS logging

The location of waste zones is recorded for monitoring and reporting.

07

Monitoring platform

Collected data can be shared with a monitoring dashboard for analysis.

Technologies Used

Built with practical electronics and environmental intelligence.

Arduino UNO

Acts as the central microcontroller to coordinate system operations and control logic.

ESP32

Enables wireless communication, processing, and smart connectivity for the buoy.

AI-based waste detection

Improves the system’s ability to recognize floating waste from visual information.

GPS module

Tracks buoy locations and records polluted zones with position data.

pH sensor

Measures the water’s acidity or alkalinity to assess water quality.

Turbidity sensor

Detects suspended particles and pollution levels in the water.

Water temperature sensor

Monitors thermal changes that can influence aquatic conditions and pollution patterns.

GSM module

Supports communication and remote data transmission for monitoring.

BLDC motors

Drive the buoy and collection mechanism efficiently with reliable motion control.

Net-based collection mechanism

Physically gathers floating waste from the river surface in an environmentally focused way.
