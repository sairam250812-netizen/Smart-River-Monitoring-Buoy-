01 / THE WHY

A river is a living system

Healthy waterways
need our attention.

Floating litter can travel far, harm wildlife, and break down into smaller pieces that are difficult to remove. Traditional cleanup is important, but teams also need better ways to spot changing conditions and understand where attention is needed.

RiverGuard AI explores how connected sensors and AI-assisted vision could help make river care more informed, timely, and focused.

The parts behind the idea

Technology with
a purpose.

A mix of familiar electronics and sensing tools could help the buoy observe, decide, and communicate.

⌘CONTROL

Arduino UNO + ESP32

The UNO can coordinate control tasks. The ESP32 adds wireless connectivity and can support data exchange between system components.

MicrocontrollerConnectivity
◉PERCEPTION

Camera + AI vision

Image analysis can help recognize likely floating waste. Detection quality depends on camera views and training data.

⌖LOCATION

GPS module

Records the buoy's location so observations can be connected to places along the river.

≈WATER SENSING

pH + turbidity

pH estimates acidity or alkalinity; turbidity indicates how cloudy the water is from suspended particles.

℃CONDITIONS

Temperature sensor

Measures water temperature, a useful environmental observation when interpreted with other information.

⌁COMMUNICATION

GSM module

Can send readings or status updates over cellular networks where coverage is available.

⟳MOTION

BLDC motors

Provide controlled movement for navigation or a collection mechanism, subject to the final design.

⌑COLLECTION

Net-based collection

A carefully designed net can gather suitable floating debris. It needs safe handling, maintenance, and responsible disposal.
